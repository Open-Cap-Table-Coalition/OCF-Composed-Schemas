`conversion_triggers[].trigger_date` / `conversion_triggers[].end_date`: When the source metadata explicitly identifies a trigger or date-window boundary as the instrument's maturity, should that date populate `ConvertibleNote.maturityDatetime` (and the mirrored issuance-transaction slot)?

Asked by @johnscrudato→ ConvertibleNote.maturityDatetime · objects/transactions/issuance/ConvertibleIssuance.mapping.md:318
Current answer Open: `trigger_date` is also used for scheduled conversion and `end_date` for elective conversion windows, so a generic date-to-maturity mapping would be semantically unsafe. A conditional “explicitly maturity” policy may be appropriate.
