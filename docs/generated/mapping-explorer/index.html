OCF ↔ Carta / cap-table data

How cap-table records move from OCF to Carta.

OCF is the Open Cap Format: the source-side records and events that describe a cap table. Carta is the destination data model in this comparison. Start with an OCF record to see where each field goes, or switch to Carta records to see what is mapped, derived, or still unmatched.

Every Carta definition is listed here. Mapped entries have a direct OCF record route; entries needing review lack a standalone route; support entries are nested helpers used inside another Carta record.

Target page scope

Mapped (18) have a direct OCF record route. Needs a decision (1) are gaps that need a mapping choice. No standalone record (12) are documented exclusions such as summaries, alternate shapes, or Carta-only workflows. Support (55) are nested helpers used inside other Carta records.
