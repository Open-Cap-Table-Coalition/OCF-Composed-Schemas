## Notes / open questions

- Carta has no retraction or correction transaction for convertible notes. `date`, `security_id`, and `reason_text` are therefore `no-equivalent`.
- `id`, `comments`, and `object_type` are OCF scaffolding; a retraction is not interchangeable with a security cancellation.
