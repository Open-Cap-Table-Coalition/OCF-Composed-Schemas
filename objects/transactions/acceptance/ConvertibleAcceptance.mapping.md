## Notes / open questions

- Carta has no convertible-acceptance transaction and ConvertibleNote has no acceptance-date field. The substantive `date` and `security_id` therefore have no target; do not route them to the acceptance fields on non-convertible securities.
- `id`, `comments`, and `object_type` are OCF scaffolding; Carta types supported records positionally and assigns its own identifiers.
